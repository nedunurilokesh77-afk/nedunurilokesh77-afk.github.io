# Deploy to GitHub Pages
1. Open your repo (nedunurilokesh77-afk.github.io). Delete the old files, then upload ALL files and folders from the `site` folder (index.html, style.css, main.js, scene.js, boardtex.js, contact.js, assets/, vendor/). Commit to `main`.
2. Repo > Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
3. After ~1 minute your site is live at https://nedunurilokesh77-afk.github.io/
To update later: edit or upload the changed files and commit again.
