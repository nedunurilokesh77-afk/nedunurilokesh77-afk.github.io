I’m Lokesh Nedunuri, a second-year B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering student at VIT-AP University.

I’m interested in understanding electronic systems from the circuit level to processor-based systems. My academic project experience includes 8085 and 8086 microprocessor platforms, along with ARM Cortex-M3 / LPC1768-based work. My practical exposure also includes transistors, diodes, MOSFETs, PCB design and PCB fabrication.

I have used MATLAB, Arduino IDE, Keil µVision, Multisim, Xilinx Vivado, KiCad and EasyEDA during my ECE learning. I enjoy analytical thinking, logical reasoning, circuit analysis, self-learning, research and experimentation, and team collaboration. I’m continuing to explore different areas of ECE while building a strong engineering foundation.
